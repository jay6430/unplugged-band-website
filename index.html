About the band

Loud enough to move a crowd.
Soft enough to move you.

The Unplugged Band started the way most good things do — a few friends, a room full of instruments, and no plan beyond playing the songs they loved. That room turned into stages: monsoon festivals in Saputara, Navratri grounds, weddings, college fests and corporate nights across multiple cities.

Our set moves between three worlds. Bollywood for the singalongs everybody already knows. Gujarati folk for the moment the whole floor gets up. And fusion for the in-between — the unplugged, stripped-back arrangements that make a big room feel small and personal.

  • ♪ Full live band — vocals, guitars, keys, drums & percussion
  • ♪ Custom setlists built around your event and your crowd
  • ♪ Travels across Gujarat and beyond, sound & setup sorted
  • ♪ Unplugged acoustic sets or full high-energy production
